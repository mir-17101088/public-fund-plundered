One family, eight firms and Tk 1200 cr plundering of public funds

Eight development projects went to firms owned by one family. Documents show payments far ahead of the work, missing records, and a road that is mostly paid for but not yet begun.

Three years into signing the contract, construction of the 10.6km road in Pirojpur’s Bhandaria Upazila has not even begun.

Yet, the contractor has already been paid Tk 13.23 crore of the Tk 15.92 crore work order, or over 80 percent of the contract value.

This is only one example of alleged plundering of taxpayer money by a clique of government officials, contracting firms and influential politicians, including two members of parliament in the district.

The Pirojpur district Local Government Engineering Department (LGED) has investigated 17 development projects undertaken between 2020 and 2024, and found evidence of corruption and embezzlement amounting to Tk 1,195 crore in at least eight projects.

But these projects share more than a pattern of corruption by random contractors. All eight projects in question went to eight firms owned by the same family: former Pirojpur-2 MP Mohiuddin Maharaj, his three brothers and a sister-in-law.

Documents show a series of fund misappropriations through premature disbursement to these contractors, multiple payments made for the same work, use of substandard construction materials and irregularities in official recordkeeping. In some cases, official records were nowhere to be found.

Each bar is one of the eight projects, drawn against its contract value. Grey is the share of the work done, by the LGED’s figures.

Red is what was paid on top of that. In all eight projects, the amount paid was higher than the value of the work done.

One contractor was paid out Tk 996 crore against the approved project cost of Tk 936 crore — Tk 60 crore more than the contract value, according to an August 2026 LGED monitoring report.

The scheme — Southern Country Iron Bridge Reconstruction and Rehabilitation Project for the improvement of connectivity in 42 upazilas in Barisal — was scheduled for completion by June 2026. On the ground, however, only half the work has been completed.

Similarly, the Barishal Division Important Upazila and Union Road Widening and Strengthening Project was launched in 2022, with an extended deadline for completion by June 2027. The LGED report shows that Tk 624 crore — 89 percent of the contract value — has been disbursed already, with only 15 percent of the work done so far.

The Daily Star’s analysis of the project documents shows the combined contract value of the eight projects is Tk 2,141 crore. Based on the percentage of work completed, Tk 826.38 crore should have been disbursed.

In reality, Tk 2,069 crore has already been released, around Tk 1,243 crore more than the cost of the work completed.

Visiting the site of the planned 10.6 km road in Pirojpur’s Bhandaria upazila in August, The Daily Star found that sections of the route were in particularly poor condition.

Montu Hawlader, a regular biker, said that the condition of the road is so bad that he fell off his motorcycle six times in recent months. He escaped unhurt as there was no traffic on the road at those times, he said.

The road in Bhandaria upazila, photographed from above at one spot as traffic passed.

The clique

The contractor of the road in question is EFTE. ETCL (Pvt.) Limited, whose managing director is Merajul Islam, brother of former Pirojpur-2 MP Mohiuddin Maharaj.

Including this, he owns four of the firms that won the projects where corruption and embezzlement were found.

The three others are EFTE Enterprise, EFTE Trading Corporation Limited and South Bangla Trading Limited.

A fifth, Shimu Enterprise, belongs to his wife, Shamima Akhter. The sixth company, Horinpala Trade International, is owned by Mohiuddin Maharaj himself.

The remaining two projects went to his two younger brothers’ firms — M/S Teli Khali Construction, owned by Md Shamsuddin, and Ishan Enterprises, owned by Mohammad Salahuddin, according to an investigation by the Anti-Corruption Commission.

Two of these companies — M/S Teli Khali Construction and EFTE. ETCL (Pvt.) Limited — together secured nearly 375 contracts from the office of the executive engineer of Pirojpur LGED worth more than Tk 1,000 crore.

They were the only bidders in nearly 200 of the contracts, meaning no other contractors were allowed to even participate, said Mohammad Tauhidul Islam, who studies public procurement at Transparency International Bangladesh (TIB).

The official added that the nature and level of corruption points to the involvement of top government officials, the ministry and local offices.

A separate LGED investigation report from January 2025 came to the same conclusion. It found that the financial irregularities and embezzlement “was not possible without the direct support of politically influential figures at the local and national levels and the highest-ranking officials at the policy level of the previous [Awami League] government.”

The LGED report names:

  • SM Rezaul KarimFormer Pirojpur-1 MP and minister
  • Mohiuddin MaharajFormer Pirojpur-2 MP
  • Merajul IslamFormer Sadar Upazila Chairman
  • Nure Alam ShahinFormer Nazirpur Upazila Chairman
  • Md Tofazzel Hossain MiahFormer principal secretary at the Prime Minister’s Office

In his statement to the investigation committee, AKM Mozammel Hoque Khan, accounts officer of the LGED’s Pirojpur office, also pointed to collusion between contracting firms, politicians, and officials. He specifically named former executive engineer Abdus Sattar Howlader, who approved these bills.

Md Azizur Rahman, current LGED executive engineer in Pirojpur, told The Daily Star that all eight contractors have since been blacklisted and that the projects under these licences will be cancelled upon approval by the chief engineer. He added that they are yet to receive any instructions from the head office regarding resumption of these projects.

Documents vanished

LGED investigations and a recent audit also noted irregularities in paperwork and recordkeeping.

In 2025, the Office of the Comptroller and Auditor General raised audit objections of Tk 2,666 crore across the 17 projects.

The report cites cases of bridges constructed without connecting approach roads, and bills submitted for the same work under multiple projects.

For example, under the Supporting Rural Bridges Programme, a contract worth Tk 21.42 lakh was signed with EFTE. ETCL (Pvt.) Limited. However, a cheque for Tk 15.04 crore was issued to Rupali Construction instead.

The report notes that officials at the District Accounts and Finance Officer’s Office failed to produce the bills, vouchers and other supporting documents to explain these discrepancies.

Several files were found to only contain note sheets signed by the accountant and the executive engineer, with no corroborating signatures from other officials.

Speaking to the LGED investigation team in December 2024, Accountant AKM Mozammel Hoque Khan stated that these documents were kept with the contractors on instructions of the executive engineers.

He added that former Executive Engineer Abdus Sattar Howlader asked him to sign irregular bills.

Under ACC scanner

Last year, the Anti-Corruption Commission filed eight cases against 27 people in relation to these irregularities. The accused include Mohiuddin Maharaj, three of his brothers including Merajul Islam, Merajul’s wife, and 22 officials from the local LGED and the District Accounts Office.

Charges include the withdrawal of Tk 1,080 crore from several schemes under the eight projects doing only partial work.

The money, according to the ACC, was primarily withdrawn through eight contracting firms registered in the names of Mohiuddin Maharaj and his family members.

Six of the accused officials were arrested in connection with the case.

Among them, the accounts officer of the LGED Pirojpur office, AKM Mozammel Hoque Khan, died in jail, while five officials from the Accounts Office are out on bail.

The rest of the accused are fugitives, said Md Aminul Islam, deputy director at ACC’s Pirojpur district office.

In March 2026, the ACC filed nine more cases over accumulation of assets beyond known income sources against Mohiuddin Maharaj, Abdus Sattar Howlader, and their family members.

The cases are currently under investigation.

Separately, the ACC has seized assets worth about Tk 300 crore belonging to the accused in Pirojpur and Dhaka, Md Aminul Islam said.

What they said

Contacted by phone, SM Rezaul Karim said he was not a minister or a contractor during the period, and had no authority over LGED.

But records show that in a cabinet reshuffle in February 2020, Rezaul was made minister for fisheries and livestock. Earlier, he was minister for housing and public works.

Nure Alam Shahin, who was elected chairman in 2024, denied that he or his brother (SM Rezaul Karim) was involved in fund embezzlement.

Mohiuddin Maharaj said he was elected as an independent MP only in January 2024, and served for seven months. He said he held no significant position at the time.

Merajul Islam denied all allegations, adding that many of the projects reported as incomplete have actually been completed.

Acknowledging some excess payments to avoid lapse of allocated funds at the end of the fiscal year, he claimed no money was withdrawn without doing any work.

He added that he still wanted to complete the projects, and applied for permission to complete the work, and is prepared to adjust for the excess payments.

The Daily Star could not contact Md Tofazzel Hossain Miah and Abdus Sattar Howlader for comments, as their phone numbers were found switched off.
